Hodai is a free, open source music player for desktop and mobile. Open it, search for a song, and press play. There is no account to create and nothing to configure first: the music source is set up already, using public and free music metadata providers and the YouTube Music API.

Features:
* Synced lyrics that follow the song
* Downloadable tracks for offline listening
* Cross-platform: Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux
* Group listening rooms
* No ads
* No telemetry, diagnostics, or user data collection
* Playback runs locally on your device
* Plugin support for other music sources
* Open source, licensed under AGPL-3.0
